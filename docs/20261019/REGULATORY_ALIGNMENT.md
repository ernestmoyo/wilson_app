# Regulatory alignment: location certificates (19 October 2026)

Read-only review of Assure Safety Field against the current HSW (Hazardous Substances)
Regulations 2017, the Location Compliance Certification Performance Standard 2021 (LCC PS),
and the Information and Process Requirements Performance Standard 2019 as amended in 2023
(IPS). Branch `feat/graph-model`. No code changed. Clause text was read from the documents
listed under Sources. Where something was not verified, the table says so.

Two naming corrections for `docs/GRAPH.md` and the code comments:
- The location standard is the **LCC Performance Standard 2021** (in force 1 April 2022).
  "WKS-17" is the workbooks' own label, not WorkSafe's.
- **IPS clause 11 was replaced** by the Amendment Performance Standard 2023 (in force
  1 May 2023). It now requires refusal notices within 15 working days and lists 13 fields
  each notice must contain.

The 2.1.2 aerosol thresholds used by B6 are all measured in **aggregate water capacity** (litres):
- **Above 3,000 L:** a hazardous substance location is required, and so a certificate (Schedule 9 table 4, 2.1.2A).
- **Up to 10,000 L:** reg 11.24.
- **10,000 to 100,000 L:** reg 11.25.
- **Above 100,000 L:** reg 11.26. This band is beyond Appendix A.

The LCC PS Schedule 2 cl 9 action is "establish the quantity of aerosols present and confirm the aggregate water capacity exceeds 3,000 L".

## 3. What did and did not change, 2024 to October 2026

- **Regulations** (latest version as at 23 September 2026):
  - **20 Oct 2025** (SL 2025/199): reg 11.32(1)(a)(ii) and 11.33(1A)-(1D), retail shops.
  - **30 Jul 2026** (SL 2026/207): reg 1.11, reg 10.1, **10.6(1)(a)** (hazardous area standard is now the 2022 edition) with transitional Sch 1 cl 55-56; Part 18 (laboratories).
  - **23 Sep 2026:** class 1 / arms-licence references (reg 9.5, 9.6, 9.8). Not relevant here.
  - **No change was found** to Part 6 (regs 6.22-6.28), reg 8.1/8.2, 10.34, 10.36, 12.17/12.18, 13.38/13.39 or 17.91.
- **Performance standards:**
  - **LCC PS 2021** is still the current location standard on WorkSafe's performance-standards page.
  - **IPS 2019** is amended only by the **Amendment PS 2023** (clause 11, refusals).
  - No newer location standard was found.
- **Safe work instruments added since 2024:**
  - Requirements for Approving a Type of Burner, SWI 2025 (in force 30 June 2025). Burners are C8-excluded.
  - Periodic Test Interval for Seamless Steel Cylinders, SWI 2025. Relevant to cylinder work, not locations.
  - **Validity Periods of Compliance Certificates for SCS, SWI 2018** sets the 17.91 certificate validity. Read it before building the SCS phase. Not reviewed here.
- **WorkSafe guidance:** "Obligations when issuing certificates and refusals" (current page). It covers:
  - A 6.23(3)(a) certificate must state that regulation and the requirement not met.
  - A **risk assessment is required** before any 6.23(3)(a) or 6.24 certificate.
  - Hazardous atmosphere zones, segregation and ignition control are **never minor**.
  - At **4 or more** minor failures, consider refusing (LCC PS cl 15(2)).
  - Help a PCBU for no more than **20 working days** before deciding.
  - Make one refusal notice listing every unmet requirement.

  The app has no `6.23(3)(a)` decision type and no risk-assessment record. **Gap, P2:** add the decision "issued under 6.23(3)(a)", which requires an unmet requirement plus a risk-assessment note. Warn when there are 4 or more non-compliances on a conditional decision. Block a conditional decision when a hazardous-area, segregation or ignition item is non-compliant.
